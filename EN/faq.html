📌 Professional Canvas Printing FAQ

General Information
About Canvas
What is canvas printing?
Canvas printing is the process of printing photos or designs on high-quality artistic canvas, giving it the look of a painting.
Is printing on canvas different from a regular photo?
Yes. Canvas has a fabric base with depth and texture that creates a painting effect, while regular photos are printed on flat paper.
How long does canvas printing last?
With proper care (dry place, no direct sunlight), canvas prints can last 70–100 years.
Does canvas printing fade under sunlight?
No, if UV-protected inks are used. However, prolonged direct sunlight may reduce color saturation.
Is it possible to wash a printed canvas?
No, washing is not recommended. Clean with a dry soft cloth or a duster.
Materials and Quality
What material is canvas made of?
Canvas is usually made of cotton or polyester. Cotton gives a natural look, polyester provides smoother and brighter colors.
What type of ink is used for canvas printing?
Eco-solvent or pigment inks are used, which are water-resistant and long-lasting.
Is canvas thickness important?
Yes. Thicker canvas ensures durability and longer lifespan.
Does canvas crack over time?
No, if printed on quality materials and stored properly.
Is the surface of canvas matte or glossy?
Canvas is usually matte, but it can be coated for a glossy finish.
Printing Process
What quality of photo is needed for canvas printing?
At least 150–300 dpi resolution, depending on size.
Can you print a photo taken with a mobile phone?
Yes, if the quality is good enough. Old or pixelated images may not look clear.
Is the photo edited before printing?
Yes, photos are often color-corrected to improve quality.
Will the printed colors look the same as on the screen?
Canvas has a natural texture, so colors may slightly differ from what you see on screen.
How are the edges of the canvas finished?
You can choose either gallery wrap (image continues on edges) or white borders.
Sizes and Formats
What is the minimum canvas size?
Usually starts from 20x20 cm.
What is the maximum canvas size?
Depending on equipment – up to 150 cm width, length is nearly unlimited.
Can you print panoramas?
Yes, large panoramic canvases are possible.
Can you print custom sizes?
Yes, we print according to customer requirements.
Can one photo be split across multiple canvases?
Yes, this is called “multi-panel printing.”
Placement and Use
Can I use a nail to hang the canvas?
Yes, canvases are usually stretched on a wooden frame and ready to hang.
Does the canvas come framed?
No, we provide only the printed canvas.
Is canvas heavy?
No, it is lightweight and easy to hang.
Can canvas be used outdoors?
No, not recommended. Moisture and sun may damage it.
Can canvas be placed in the kitchen or bathroom?
Not recommended in humid or smoky environments.

Yes, in some cases even same-day orders are possible.

Yes, it is securely packaged to avoid damage.
Do you ship outside the city?
Yes, we deliver throughout Armenia.

Yes, multiple photos can be combined on one canvas.

Yes, if the quality is good and it does not violate copyright.

Yes, with design editing.

Care and Maintenance
How should I clean a canvas?
Clean with a soft dry cloth. Do not use water or chemicals.
Can I dust the canvas?
Yes, with a gentle vacuum mode.
Can I transport the canvas rolled up?
Yes, but it is better to transport it stretched on a frame and packaged.
Does the canvas sag over time?
No, a quality wooden frame prevents this issue.
Can a damaged canvas be reprinted?
You cannot reprint on the same canvas, but we can print a new one from your file.
